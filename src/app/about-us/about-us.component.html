What Drives Us

Our Mission

Empower small and medium grocery store owners with modern digital tools that are affordable, easy to use, and work offline — no internet required.

Our Vision

A world where every kirana shop and fresh mart in India runs on smart digital billing — eliminating lost sales, pricing errors, and manual paperwork.

Our Values

Simplicity first. We believe technology should adapt to the user, not the other way around. Every feature we build must be usable in under 10 seconds.

The Story Behind Handy Bill

Handy Bill started with a simple observation: most billing software is too complex for the typical Indian grocery store. Store owners don't have time for training, complicated setups, or expensive hardware.

We built Handy Bill to be different — a mobile-first app that any shop worker can use in minutes. Barcode scanning for instant price lookup. Pending bill tracking per customer. Daily reports. All in one simple app that works on any Android device.

Today, hundreds of stores across India trust Handy Bill for their daily billing. But we're just getting started.
